Cold water · Trout Unlimited

A deck of fish is worth less than the water they live in.

We draw trout flies for a living, which is a strange thing to do if the trout are gone. A set amount from every deck goes to Trout Unlimited, and we hold a Trout Unlimited Business membership. Here is precisely what that does and does not mean.

What we actually do

Two things, both small and both specific:

  • A per-deck contribution. A set amount from every deck sold goes to Trout Unlimited. It is a fixed figure per deck, not a share of profit, so it does not quietly become zero in a bad quarter.
  • A Trout Unlimited Business membership. We joined as a business member. That is a membership, which anyone can buy — it is not a partnership, a sponsorship, or an endorsement, and Trout Unlimited has not vouched for this product.

Why Trout Unlimited

Trout Unlimited works on cold-water habitat: reconnecting streams that culverts and old dams cut in half, restoring the shade and gravel that spawning depends on, and arguing for water policy in rooms where anglers usually aren't. It is unglamorous, long-horizon work on the exact resource this deck depends on. Every fly we drew imitates something that hatches in cold, clean, connected water — or it imitates nothing at all.

You can read their work, or join directly, at tu.org. If you'd rather send them a membership than buy a deck of cards, that is a straightforwardly better use of the money and we will not be offended.

What we are careful not to claim

  • We do not say Trout Unlimited endorses The Reel Deal Deck. It does not.
  • We do not say we are a Trout Unlimited partner. We are a business member, which is a different and smaller thing.
  • We do not say “a portion of proceeds” and leave it there. That phrase can mean a cent, and shoppers have learned to read it that way for good reason.
