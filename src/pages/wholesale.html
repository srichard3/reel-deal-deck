At the counter

Why it moves off a shelf

Every shop already knows what a slow-turning SKU feels like. This is the other kind.

It sits in the impulse band

At a $24.95 shelf price it is an add-on, not a decision. Customers who came in for leader material do not go home to think about it. That is the single most important thing about the price, and it is why we did not put it at $39.

It is gift-ready with no work from you

Flat, light, sized like a deck of cards, no sizing, no returns for fit, no batteries and no expiry. November through December it solves the same problem for every non-angler who walks into your shop looking lost.

It is a conversation piece

Original hand-drawn flies with what each one imitates. Customers open the box on the counter and start arguing about patterns. That conversation is what actually sells the second deck, and it costs your staff nothing to start.

It does not go stale

No seasons, no model years, no hatch chart to date it. A deck that does not sell in March is worth exactly the same in October, which makes carrying two cases a much easier decision than carrying two of anything else.

It shelves anywhere

Counter tray, register spinner, gift wall, or a stack next to the fly bins. A brick of twelve takes roughly the footprint of one fly box, which is the cheapest inventory real estate in the shop.

It teaches, which sells flies

Someone who understands what a Pheasant Tail imitates buys more of them. A deck that makes beginners literate about patterns is not competing with your fly bins, it is feeding them.

Who we are opening accounts for

  • Fly shops

    Counter and gift-wall placement. Start with two cases, reorder in twelves. The customer who buys one in June is the customer who buys four in December.

  • Guide services

    End-of-day gift, client thank-you, or a branded add-on to a full-day trip. Cheaper than a hat and considerably more likely to be kept.

  • Lodges and outfitters

    In the room, in the welcome pack, or in the pro shop. Guests who are new to the water use it the first evening, which is the point.

  • Trout Unlimited chapters

    Banquet tables, raffles and membership drives. A portion of every deck goes to Trout Unlimited, so it is on-mission as well as on-budget.

  • Corporate and brand gifting

    Outdoor brands, dealer incentives, conference gifts and client thank-yous. Larger quantities than the table below — tell us the number and the date and we will quote it.

  • Museums, clubs and events

    Casting clubs, fishing museums, expo booths and festival merch tables. Small orders welcome — the minimum is deliberately low.
