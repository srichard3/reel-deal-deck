Why it lands

The rare gift you can buy for an expert without knowing anything

Nothing to size, match or guess

No rod weight. No reel that has to balance. No waders that have to fit. A deck of cards is one of the very few things in this sport where there is no wrong answer to buy.

It is genuinely useful on the water

Every card says what that fly imitates, in plain language. It settles the "what do I tie on" question at the truck. Useful beats decorative, every time, with people who actually do the thing.

It is a real object

Genuine Bicycle / USPCC stock with the patented Air-Cushion finish, on FSC-certified paper with vegetable-based inks. Not a laminated card set in a shrink-wrapped sleeve.

It gives them something to say

Fifty-four original illustrations and a portion of every deck going to Trout Unlimited. It comes with a story attached, which is most of what a good gift is doing.

Before you decide

The five things gift-buyers ask us

Will they already have one?

Almost certainly not. There are fly-fishing card sets on the market — they are photo-printed reference cards, sold mostly through shops, and very few anglers own one.

This deck has not been printed yet, so nobody has it. If you get in early you are giving something that did not exist last year, which is exactly the opposite of the problem you have with another net or another pair of nippers.

I do not know anything about fly fishing. Can I still pick this?

Yes, and that is rather the point. You do not have to guess a hook size, a rod weight, a line taper, or a reel that matches the one they already own.

Fifty-four classic flies with what each one imitates is useful to a beginner and interesting to somebody who has fished for thirty years. There is no size to get wrong and nothing to check for compatibility. If you want to sound like you knew what you were doing, read a few entries in the Fly Library first — it is free and it takes five minutes.

Is this a real gift or a gag gift?

A real one. It is a full 54-card poker deck printed on genuine Bicycle / USPCC stock with the patented Air-Cushion finish, on FSC-certified paper with vegetable-based inks, and every fly on it was drawn by hand.

It is priced at $24 because it is made like something a person keeps, not like a novelty tin from a checkout counter. If it were a gag gift we would have printed it on the cheap board and charged nine dollars.

Will it arrive in time for Christmas or a birthday?

Not right now, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. The deck is not in production yet, there is no checkout on this site, and no order can be placed here today.

If you need a gift this week, this is not it, and we would rather you knew that than found out after a confirmation email. Join the list and we will email you when decks go on sale, with enough notice to plan around a date.

What if they do not like it?

It is a deck of cards. Worst case it lives in a boat bag and gets played with on a rained-out afternoon. That is a much softer landing than a rod they have to pretend to like.

Low downside is the entire reason a deck is one of the few genuinely safe things to buy for an expert.
