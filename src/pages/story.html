A couple of things we won’t claim

We would love to say nobody else makes fly-fishing cards. We can’t — several companies do, and one search would catch us out. What we will stand behind is narrower and checkable: the art is Ken’s and drawn by hand, the stock is genuine Bicycle from {{ site.product.manufacturer }}, and a set amount from every deck goes to {{ site.conservation.partner }}.

No founding legend either. No golden evening on a riverbank. A man who wanted to keep up with his friends, and his daughter who thought he was onto something.
