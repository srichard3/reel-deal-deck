Why a deck of cards has a conservation page at all

We draw trout flies for a living, which is a strange thing to do if the trout are gone. Every fly in this deck imitates something that hatches in cold, clean, connected water — or it imitates nothing at all. The habitat is the product, one step removed.

So this was settled before the first card was finished, not bolted on afterwards. We are grateful to be able to give something back from the very beginning, while the whole thing is still two people and a stack of drawings.

{{ site.motto }} That line is printed in the deck too, and it is the plainest argument for catch-and-release we know how to make: the fish you let go is the one somebody else gets to find.

What we actually do

Two things, both small and both specific:

  • A per-deck contribution. A set amount from every deck sold goes to {{ site.conservation.partner }}. It is a fixed figure per deck, not a share of profit, so it does not quietly become zero in a bad quarter.
  • A {{ site.conservation.partner }} Business membership. We joined as a business member, and the mark is printed on the info card inside the deck. That is a membership, which anyone can buy — it is not a partnership, a sponsorship or an endorsement, and {{ site.conservation.partner }} has not vouched for this product.

Why {{ site.conservation.partner }}

{{ site.conservation.partner }} works on cold-water habitat: reconnecting streams that culverts and old dams cut in half, restoring the shade and gravel that spawning depends on, and arguing for water policy in rooms where anglers usually aren’t. It is unglamorous, long-horizon work on the exact resource this deck depends on.

You can read their work, or join directly, at tu.org. If you would rather send them a membership than buy a deck of cards, that is a straightforwardly better use of the money and we will not be offended.

What we are careful not to claim

  • We do not say {{ site.conservation.partner }} endorses The Reel Deal Deck. It does not.
  • We do not say we are a {{ site.conservation.partner }} partner. We are a business member, which is a different and smaller thing.
  • We do not say “a portion of proceeds” and leave it there. That phrase can mean a cent, and shoppers have learned to read it that way for good reason.
  • We do not claim the deck itself is carbon-neutral, plastic-free or climate-positive. It is paper, ink and glue, and the sheet below says which.
